The problem today

Compliance for thousands of entities, tracked by hand.

NITDA enforces data protection and digital compliance across Nigeria's digital economy. Internally, much of that tracking still runs on spreadsheets, email threads and ad hoc reviews.

Manual tracking

Records for regulated entities live in spreadsheets and inboxes, maintained one update at a time.

No live visibility

Leadership cannot see who is compliant, due or overdue at any given moment.

Enforcement gaps

Missed deadlines surface only at the next manual review, often weeks too late.

No prioritisation

Without a risk score, officers have no standard way to decide who to chase first.

Why it matters

NITDA cannot credibly enforce digital compliance on Nigeria's technology sector while managing that same enforcement manually.

How CORE works

Four modules. One closed loop.

Entities are registered, deadlines calculate themselves, the flagging engine runs continuously, and officers work from a ranked queue.

04 · ENFORCEMENT QUEUE

The highest risk sits at the top.

A 0–100 risk score weighs days overdue, data-subject tier, sector sensitivity, prior violations and unanswered notices. Every action taken is logged with officer and timestamp.

03

Pillar 3: Strengthen policy implementation & legal framework

CORE turns NITDA's regulatory mandates into an automated, evidence-backed enforcement system, in step with the agency's AI Transformation Roadmap.

Closes the enforcement gap

No entity slips through undetected. NDPA, GAID and CAR mandates are checked continuously, not at the next review.

Strengthens legal standing

A complete, timestamped audit trail makes every enforcement decision documented, consistent and defensible in a dispute.

Enables evidence-based regulation

Leadership sees compliance by sector, region and severity today, instead of in a report from three months ago.

Roadmap to deployment

Prototype to agency-wide in three phases.

The architecture is modular, so each phase can be scoped and funded on its own.
